# Assessment 1 - Grade 7 Fractions and Decimals

Type: homework | Total marks: 12 | All content is synthetic.

## Question 1 (2 marks) - Topic: Fractions
Calculate 3/4 + 2/3. Give your answer as a mixed number.

**Model answer:** 3/4 + 2/3 = 9/12 + 8/12 = 17/12 = 1 5/12

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: common denominator of 12 with both fractions converted correctly
- 1 mark: 1 5/12 as a mixed number (17/12 left improper does not get this mark)

## Question 2 (2 marks) - Topic: Fractions
Calculate 2/5 x 15/8. Simplify fully.

**Model answer:** (2 x 15)/(5 x 8) = 30/40 = 3/4

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: multiplies numerators and denominators, or cancels correctly first
- 1 mark: 3/4 fully simplified

## Question 3 (3 marks) - Topic: Decimals
Convert 3/8 to a decimal and to a percentage. Show your working.

**Model answer:** 3 / 8 = 0.375, so 37.5%

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: division method shown
- 1 mark: 0.375
- 1 mark: 37.5% (follow through from their decimal)

## Question 4 (2 marks) - Topic: Ordering
Put in order, smallest first: 0.65, 5/8, 3/5, 0.61

**Model answer:** 3/5 = 0.6 and 5/8 = 0.625, so 3/5, 0.61, 5/8, 0.65

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: converts both fractions to decimals (0.6 and 0.625)
- 1 mark: correct order

## Question 5 (3 marks) - Topic: Word problems
A pizza is cut into 12 equal slices. One friend eats 1/4 of it and another eats 1/3 of it. What fraction of the pizza is left?

**Model answer:** 1/4 + 1/3 = 3/12 + 4/12 = 7/12 eaten, so 1 - 7/12 = 5/12 left (5 slices)

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: adds the two fractions correctly (7/12)
- 1 mark: subtracts from the whole (1 or 12/12)
- 1 mark: 5/12 stated as a fraction (an answer of "5 slices" alone does not get this mark)
