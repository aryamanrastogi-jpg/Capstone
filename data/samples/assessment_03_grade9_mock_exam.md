# Assessment 3 - Grade 9 Mock Exam: Geometry, Percentages and Probability

Type: mock exam | Total marks: 18 | Calculator allowed | All content is synthetic.

## Question 1 (3 marks) - Topic: Pythagoras
A right-angled triangle has shorter sides 6 cm and 8 cm. Find the length of the hypotenuse.

**Model answer:** c^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100, so c = 10 cm

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: states or uses c^2 = a^2 + b^2
- 1 mark: 100
- 1 mark: 10 cm

## Question 2 (3 marks) - Topic: Trigonometry
In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite angle x is 7 cm and the hypotenuse is 12 cm. Find x to 1 decimal place.

**Model answer:** sin x = 7/12, x = sin^-1(7/12) = 35.7 degrees

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: chooses sine (SOH)
- 1 mark: sin x = 7/12
- 1 mark: 35.7 degrees

## Question 3 (3 marks) - Topic: Percentages
A jacket costs $80. Its price is increased by 15%, then the new price is reduced by 15% in a sale. Find the sale price. Is it the same as the original price?

**Model answer:** 80 x 1.15 = 92, 92 x 0.85 = 78.20. Sale price $78.20, which is less than $80 because the 15% decrease is taken from a larger amount.

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: $92 after the increase
- 1 mark: $78.20
- 1 mark: says it is not the same, with a reason

## Question 4 (3 marks) - Topic: Circles

**Marking criteria:**

- 1 mark: pi x r^2 used
- 1 mark: 78.5 cm^2

## Question 5 (3 marks) - Topic: Probability
A bag has 4 red, 5 blue and 3 green counters. One counter is taken at random.
(a) Find P(blue). (b) Find P(not green).

**Model answer:** Total 12. (a) 5/12. (b) 1 - 3/12 = 9/12 = 3/4

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: total of 12 used
- 1 mark: (a) 5/12
- 1 mark: (b) 9/12 or 3/4

## Question 6 (3 marks) - Topic: Simultaneous equations
Solve x + y = 11 and x - y = 3.

**Model answer:** Adding: 2x = 14, x = 7. Then 7 + y = 11, y = 4.

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: eliminates one variable correctly
- 1 mark: x = 7
- 1 mark: y = 4
