# Assessment 2 - Grade 8 Linear Equations and Expressions

Type: exercise | Total marks: 15 | All content is synthetic.

## Question 1 (2 marks) - Topic: Expanding brackets
Expand and simplify 3(x - 4) - 2(x + 1).

**Model answer:** 3x - 12 - 2x - 2 = x - 14

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: both brackets expanded correctly, including -2 x +1 = -2
- 1 mark: x - 14

## Question 2 (3 marks) - Topic: Solving equations
Solve 5x + 7 = 2x + 22.

**Model answer:** 5x - 2x = 22 - 7, so 3x = 15, so x = 5

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: collects x terms on one side (3x)
- 1 mark: collects numbers on the other side (15)
- 1 mark: x = 5

## Question 3 (3 marks) - Topic: Equations with brackets
Solve 4(2x - 3) = 20.

**Model answer:** 8x - 12 = 20, so 8x = 32, so x = 4 (or divide by 4 first: 2x - 3 = 5, 2x = 8, x = 4)

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: expands correctly (8x - 12) or divides both sides by 4 correctly
- 1 mark: 8x = 32 or 2x = 8
- 1 mark: x = 4

## Question 4 (3 marks) - Topic: Forming equations
The perimeter of a rectangle is 46 cm. Its length is 5 cm more than its width. Find the width.

**Model answer:** Let width = w, length = w + 5. 2w + 2(w + 5) = 46, so 4w + 10 = 46, 4w = 36, w = 9 cm

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: forms a correct equation for the perimeter
- 1 mark: simplifies to 4w + 10 = 46 (or equivalent)
- 1 mark: width = 9 cm

## Question 5 (2 marks) - Topic: Substitution
Find the value of 2a^2 - 3b when a = -3 and b = 4.

**Model answer:** 2 x (-3)^2 - 3 x 4 = 2 x 9 - 12 = 18 - 12 = 6

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: (-3)^2 = 9 used correctly
- 1 mark: 6

## Question 6 (2 marks) - Topic: Inequalities
Solve 3x - 5 < 10 and show the answer on a number line (describe it in words).

**Model answer:** 3x < 15, so x < 5. Open circle at 5 with an arrow pointing left.

**Marking criteria:**
- 1 mark: x < 5
- 1 mark: open (not filled) circle at 5, arrow to the left
