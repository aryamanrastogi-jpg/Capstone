Camp Prep AI interface

AI study support for Cambridge IGCSE maths. Students upload work they have already done, see where they are weak, and work through a short study camp. Teachers review and approve every AI score. About 85% of the interface is for students.

Student

Lands on My Work

My study
  • My WorkUpload work and get an instant estimate. Questions you get right drop out of the next attempt (10 attempts at most). Compare your attempts side by side.
  • My QuestionsType up or import a worksheet, then work through it. Answers are optional.
  • Study CampA 3 to 14 day plan built from your weakest topics, measured against a fixed baseline.
  • Mock ExamA timed paper: 2, 3 or 4 minutes a question depending on difficulty. Compared with your camp baseline.

Interface rules

  1. AI output is a recommendation. Every student score is labelled an estimate until a teacher approves it.
  2. Hints, not answers. Students never see model answers. A practice answer appears only after they try the question.
  3. State shows as a badge as well as a number, so a teacher can see what needs attention at a glance.
  4. Mismatches are neutral. The panel says the two marks disagree. It never says the teacher was wrong.
  5. Friendly for 12 to 15 year olds. Soft 16px corners, rounded type, and short labels in plain words.

Review states

⏳ Awaiting reviewAI score. Counts for nothing yet.
✓ ApprovedTeacher accepted it unchanged.
✎ Edited by teacherScore or wording changed.
⚑ FlaggedKept out of analytics. Never becomes a score.
